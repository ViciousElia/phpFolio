## Destined (maybe)

A short love poem. It's cheesy and not profound. It's heartfelt and cliche. And it says what I want to say. And it feels good. And hopefully it is forever.

---

<div class="subframe" style="width:fit-content;margin:auto">
Had I the time and the promise  
Had I the life and assurance  
Had I simply the knowledge that I could

I would await your arrival

In this or any life  
In this or any world  
In any possible circumstance

I would await your arrival

Your smile outshines the sun  
Your eyes outgleam the stars  
You outstand all around you

And I count myself lucky

I cannot ignore your presence  
I cannot detest your essence  
I can only embrace your being

And I count myself lucky

There is nought but you and I  
There is nought but love and life  
There is only the joy of us

And in all I do, I love

Only one soul  
Only one being  
Only one woman

And that, love, is you.
</div>
