## Twilit Fantasy

A short piece that was inspired by the quickly setting sun in a beautiful place with a beautiful woman and the way she makes my heart melt. As always, I am a cheeseball, and I am more than okay with that. I will be this way till I croak, with any luck.

---

<div class="subframe centered">

The sun sets between the rocks  
The light fades as she finds respite  
Your eyes gleam in the twilight

My heart seems to flutter  
Is this the moment, is this how I go  
or simply the thing they call love

The sky darkens rapidly  
Your silhouette is all that holds me  
tethered to this moment, this life

The moon is gone tonight  
Hiding behind the clouds, no doubt  
The cool air turns sharp

Then I feel it  
The thing that returns light and warmth  
Flutters return to every part of me

As you take my hand  
It is not death that calls  
This is more than a moment

This is love.

</div>
