## The Expanse

A poem about feeling tiny and infinite at the same time, while actively recognising the value of those feelings. The importance of knowing how small we are, but how big the world could be.

---

<div class="content">
<div class="column">
<p>Don&rsquo;t look up!</p><p>
You might find yourself</p><p>
Not in some therapeutic</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>existential</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>experiential</p><p>
way</p><p>
You simply might</p><p>
find yourself</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>here</p><p>
In this world we share</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>or should share</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>and could share</p><p>
But more than that</p><p>
You might find yourself</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>dreaming</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>wishing</p><p>
<span class="s3"></span>loving</p><p>
Hoping for a world, a life, a dream</p><p>
Something bigger</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>something grander</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>something more</p><p>
And what happens then?</p><p>
Well that&rsquo;s not for me</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>for him or her</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>for them</p><p>
It&rsquo;s all for you</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>for here</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>for now</p><p>
And you just might find fear</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>sadness</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>pain</p>
</div>
<div class="column">
<p>For a world of possible</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>of chance</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>of maybe</p><p>
All lost!</p><p>
But that, my friend,</p><p>
is by design</p><p>
If we all somehow</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>by some wonder</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>by some miracle</p><p>
Were able for just once</p><p>
To find ourselves</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>here</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>now</p><p>
<span class="s3"></span>alive</p><p>
What marvels might we</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>as one</p><p>
discover?</p><p>
And so they</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>that is to say</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>the imperial they</p><p>
In order that we</p><p>
<span class="s1"></span>that is to say</p><p>
<span class="s2"></span>the infinite</p><p>
<span class="s3"></span>the beautiful</p><p>
<span class="s4"></span>the powerful we</p><p>
Might not find the truth</p><p>
Demand of us one thing</p><p>
Above all else.</p>

<p style="opacity:0">Whatever you fucking do,</p>

<p style="margin-bottom:-1.25em">Don&rsquo;t</p>
<p style="margin-bottom:-1.25em"><span class="s1"></span>Look</p>
<p style="margin-bottom:-1.25em"><span class="s2"></span>Up!</p>
