## Three Lists and a Lesson

So as not to give away the bit on the not-so-harrowing conclusion from this fun little exploration, I will simply say that what follows is an examination of a phenomenon without diving too deeply into the cause.

---

But I have to draw the picture properly.

\-----

Before we get to the good part. Take a moment. REALLY think about it. What do the games in those lists have in common within a list that is different between lists. If you've already figured it out, then well done. Otherwise ...

*   The last list is the most interesting one. It contains only games where the player-controlled protagonist is not only default female, but necessarily female.
*   The middle list contains two classes of games: the player-controlled protagonist is male by default, even if there are female characters involved XOR the female options are limited to specific parts of the story, and not the whole game.
*   The first list is really great because it sets you up to play male or female without restricting the gameplay in any real sense.

Now, as I mentioned, the all-female list certainly could have more games, but I only listed the ones I had available before age 18. Likewise the other two.

But here's the thing that I find interesting: in EVERY case on the first list, I chose female characters almost unilaterally. Sure, occasionally a guy would be interesting to play, but my preference was always for the girl. So far as bby Terra was concerned, it was the obvious choice. And it's interesting to note that this experience isn't exactly unique. In fact, a lot of trans content creators have made a lot of humourous content about exactly this phenomenon.

Now, I'm not saying that every little kid who plays the opposite gendered characters is definitely trans, but I am saying it is a fascinating area of study if someone wanted to run the numbers
