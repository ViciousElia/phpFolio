## Pride Month 2020 (Queer Wrath)

2020 was a rough year for us all. That was especially true from March through November. Every year, I devote some time to sharing queer content during June. I've edited it for clarity, but this is the full of what I posted that month.

---

### Day -3 (29 May)

This is the only warning I'm going to be giving. Starting June 1st, I'm going to be posting a lot of stuff related to Pride. I did this last year, and I think it's good to keep it going.

So. If you don't wanna see all that, you should consider muting me Sunday night (the 31st). I won't take any offense to it. Some folks aren't up to it. I get it. I see you.

If you do decide to follow along as I share, cool. If you wanna talk about it, cool. I have only three rules for this:

1.  Do not dismiss the experiences of others.
2.  Blatantly anti-queer comments will be deleted.
3.  DON'T BE A JERK.

### Day 1

Starting small. This is topical, and I have too many forks right now to say more.

The first Pride was a riot. One day in June 1969, police went too far, and the people started a riot in protest.

What came of the first Pride riot? A movement that helped pave the way for queer rights. That's kinda the point.

### Day 2

I'm queer. There are plenty of specifics to that, but I have reasons queer is enough. It is a legacy of progress.

We want progress. Yes, for everyone, but specifically for those who are treated worse. Because it's beyond unfair.

When I say worse in this context, I am referring to the way minorities are treated poorly in comparison to majorities, regardless of what kind of minority/majority we're covering.

As part of that, it's essential to keep intersectionality in mind.

### Day 3

We don't choose to be queer. What we choose is to be vocal about how crappy the world is. And we choose to make it better.

If we don't choose to be vocal about it, that's okay too. It's difficult to be a "crusader" or an "activist" when you spend your time struggling to survive. And we can't know what struggle other people are facing.

### Day 4

We have Pride because we're expected to be ashamed. When we're not allowed to be proud, what's the next step?

Wrath is an excellent answer. But what does that look like?

In short. Queer Wrath is going to look different person-to-person. For me, that's obnoxiously posting throughout June about all the queer stuff. Yes. All the queer stuff. For another person, that's going and leading rallies. For others still, it simply means existing and not giving in to the pressure from outside telling us constantly we don't belong.

We belong.

### Day 5

Here's a thought. If my being queer doesn't affect you (and I assure you it doesn't), why does it bother you?

Addendum: if it doesn't bother you, this question wasn't meant for you.

But be honest with yourself. Why is it that me, existing and doing no harm, bothers you so much? If your answer is "because my god", then I'll stop you there. Your religion is a restriction to you, not to me. If your answer is "I think it's gross", then I'll stop you there. You're not kissing me, you're not sleeping with me, and in most cases you're not even interacting with me. Sit down and let people live harmlessly.

### Day 6

Every day I make the choice to be visibly queer is a day I can give hope to a baby queer. So I persist. Even — and especially — on the days when I am smacked in the face with the difficulties that life brings me as a queer person.

### Day 7

Before asking someone, "How do you know you're queer?" Ask yourself, "How do I know I'm not?" I bet it'll be clear enough.

If you can't understand the reasoning, then perform the following thought experiment:

*   Picture yourself enjoying the company \[in a relationship way\] of someone of
    *   your gender experience.
    *   the opposite gender experience.
    *   indeterminate gender.
*   Think about how that makes you feel.
    *   If you were comfortable with
        *   all three? Congrats, you're queer!
        *   the first or the last? Congrats, you're queer!
        *   only the second? Congrats you're **not** queer!
    *   Else? You guessed it! You're Queer!
*   Picture yourself existing as
    *   the opposite gender experience.
    *   your gender experience.
    *   someone of indeterminate gender.
*   Think about how that makes you feel.
    *   If you were comfortable with
        *   all three? Congrats, you're queer!
        *   the first or the last? Congrats, you're queer!
        *   only the second? Congrats you're **not** queer!
    *   Else? You guessed it! You're Queer!

This is, of course, not the end-all of the queer experience, but it's definitely a starting point.

### Day 9

CW: violence against trans folks.

\-----

People sometimes ignore the things they don't experience and those that don't impact them. But as a transgender woman, I can't look away. And it hurts. The link below is a scary list. It gets worse by the year.

[https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/List\_of\_people\_killed\_for\_being\_transgender](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_people_killed_for_being_transgender)

Killing trans folks is just a subset of violent crimes against us. The larger set is pretty horrifying even without the murder.

[https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans\_bashing](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans_bashing)

What's worse is that most of those crimes go unpunished because of a really awful legal precedent. Believe it or not, it is still usable in a lot of the USA.

[https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay\_panic\_defense](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay_panic_defense)

What does all this mean, generally? That no matter how "equal" things look from the outside, we are still at a MASSIVE disadvantage. And that sucks.

### Day 12

Here's a happy one. Since starting HRT, I have felt more alive and more human than any part of my life prior.

INB4 "But you're also taking \[mental health drug\]."

Yes, I am taking \[mental health drug\], but its effects do nothing to curb dysphoria. In fact, I knew I needed \[mental health drug\] because HRT helped bring my dysphoria into manageable levels, and I was still messed up.

<Author Note: And since originally writing this, I was forced off of HRT for two months, and \[mental health drug\] was suddenly COMPLETELY ineffective. So ... yeah ... dysphoria is bigger than my mental disorder.>

Social transition alone did a lot for me, but HRT makes me \*feel\* more complete. More me. So don't come at me with nonsense.

### Day 13

Not sure how many of you saw the news yesterday, but the Gov't just revoked medical protection for trans folx.

So, minus doctors who specialise in trans care, I can now be denied regular care just cause I'm trans.

Also. INB4 "that's not true, because hippocratic oath".

It happened all the time — and once for me specifically — before the protections were enacted in the first place. Do you think it won't or can't happen again now that they've been revoked?

Where does that leave me and other trans folks? Knowing that our identity could leave us at risk?

### Day 30

Okay. Here it goes. Day 30.

**Content warning.** Mentions of transphobia, homophobia, suicidal thoughts, and other stuff, including self-abuse.

Before I get right down to it, lemme start with the important thing. I'm alive. I love myself. I love what my life has brought me. I'm in a good place despite everything stacked against me. And that has a lot to do with the support I've received over the years.

Now. On with the show.

I'm trans. I've known that actively for most of my life. I've known it innately as long as I could remember. It impacted the friendships I made, my success in school, and my path at every turn in my life. Almost every choice I've ever made was affected by it.

How does it affect choices? I've gotta pause every time I do anything to sort the consequences of what I do as a trans person. It gets to be very hard to sus out the implications of everything I do. It's made even harder by my inability to fully parse social protocol et c. So I often fail to make the best choices, despite my best efforts.

To make matters worse, everywhere I looked in "entertainment" made it clear that trans folx are not welcome in society at large. Look at shows like Maury, Montel, Jerry, and all the other daytime TV of the 90s. Add to that the serial and episodic fiction of primetime (Seinfeld has at least one transphobic joke) and the movies (Ace Ventura is super transphobic). So what was I to think about myself?

On top of being trans, my sexuality is atypical, even for a trans person. Those same sources of transphobia were constant sources of self hate for being queer in more than one way. Like, am I not getting enough already? Fuck off with that noise. And of course, I haven't even started to bring the church into it.

It was so ingrained that I started badmouthing myself by badmouthing queer folx like me. My internalised queerphobia consistently helped batter and bash my self worth. And that self-abuse was torture that made my already problematic decision making even worse.

Why do I share this?

Because it was the setup for the really bad stuff that started after I got myself out of high school and essentially on my own. My self-loathing just got worse by the day until I got so bad that I prayed for death or freedom. Neither came. While I was deployed in Iraq, I reached a point where I legitimately thought about putting the muzzle of my M4 to my chin. And I barely held on.

It took four more years of that level of pain before I started coming out to folx. And two more years to come out publicly. Hell, it was so bad in 2014, that I was hospitalised for stress and suicidal ideation. That's WHY I came out when I did. The pain was so much that hiding it wasn't worth it. If I lost folx, so be it. Better lose them than lose me.

And my story is NOT unique. It's so far from unique that queer folx are among the highest demographics for suicidal behaviour. Because it is hard not to be 100% self-loathing with everything the world throws at us. So fuck every one of you who says anything about us being messed up because we're queer. YOU are who made us this way, not our identities.

And if you ARE a bb queer, PLEASE reach out to me. Talk to someone who is NOT going to encourage self-loathing or self-abuse. Definitely not self-harm. Just the opposite. I am the one who is going to talk you up. And take care of you.

Remember. Be a hymn for good. Stop killing us indirectly. Stop killing us directly. Stop hurting us.

A spiteful Queer Wrath month to you.

End of.
